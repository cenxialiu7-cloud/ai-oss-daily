# inlineresearch/Inline-Studio

AI filmmaking on a node canvas. Build your whole visual pipeline from moodboard to final cut, and keep every render as a versioned take so you never lose the good version. Free and open source. Renders on your own GPU with the Inline Core engine (Z-Image, Krea 2, FLUX.2) or hosted models with no GPU. Train your own LoRAs on the same canvas.
