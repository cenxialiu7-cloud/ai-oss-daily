# AbhishekBarali/SpeakoFlow

Free, open-source voice dictation and AI assistant for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Offline Whisper speech-to-text — everything Wispr Flow and Superwhisper do, plus screen vision. Press a key and speak into any app. Say "Hey Flow" and it writes the reply from what's on your screen.
