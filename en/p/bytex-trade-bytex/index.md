# BYTEX-TRADE/bytex

Event-driven algorithmic trading engine for .NET. Write a strategy once and run it unchanged in backtest, paper and live trading, deterministic, with fixed-point money and adapters for Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken, Bitget, Gate, KuCoin and Hyperliquid.
