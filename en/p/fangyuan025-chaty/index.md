# Fangyuan025/Chaty

The models on your disk, put to work. A private desktop app for open models — chat, a coding agent built for small models, text-to-image, answers from your own documents, and voice. GGUF on llama.cpp, MLX on Apple Silicon. macOS, Windows, Linux. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.
