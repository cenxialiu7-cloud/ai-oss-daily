# EnragedAntelope/comfyui-identity-forge

A character forge for ComfyUI: dial in or randomize every detail - face, hair, body, wardrobe, mood - and get a coherent, reproducible person, canonically-described cosplayer, or creature as ready-to-render prompt text + JSON. Hundreds of characters and archetypes, billions of seed-stable combinations. Pure Python, no LLM, no dependencies.
