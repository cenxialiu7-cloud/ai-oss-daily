# AbrahamPaulJ/nightmare-mobile

On-device ComfyUI style node graph for Stable Diffusion on Android. Local SD1.5, SDXL, Flux, Z-image, Krea 2, text to image, img2img, inpainting and video on the Qualcomm Hexagon NPU. Fully offline, no server, no account.
